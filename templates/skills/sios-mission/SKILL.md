# SIOS Mission

Identify the authoritative instance and actual client scopes. Read the full Mission, current revision, and relevant log and evidence before making a state claim or update. A copied Mission, healthy service, or search result does not establish authority. Keep historical evidence distinct from the current state.

For an authorized update, prepare a concise complete replacement that preserves relevant outcome, completion criteria, scope, verified progress, blockers, evidence, and one executable next action. Use the configured revision-checked Mission operation and a stable unique request ID. On conflict reread and reconcile; on uncertainty follow the actual client's recovery contract. Read the full result back and verify its revision. Report missing authority, scope, or verification rather than editing the store directly. Mark completion only after every criterion and any registered completion control is satisfied.
